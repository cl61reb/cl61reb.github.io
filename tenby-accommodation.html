Tips for a scarce weekend

  • On Booking.com, tick "I'm flexible" / search ±1 day — some hosts blocked the 12th–13th but left the 11th or 14th open, and a 3-night minimum stay covering the race weekend is common.
  • Cancellations spike in the final 2–3 weeks before a sold-out event — worth rechecking daily, ideally first thing in the morning.
  • The official Ironman Wales travel page lists partner hotels who hold race-weekend blocks separately from public inventory.
  • Camping/caravan sites (Kiln Park, Trevayne Farm, Wood Park) tend to have more late availability than cottages — good fallback if you don't mind roughing it.
